What Is Epoch 1000000000?

Epoch 1000000000 is September 9, 2001, 1:46:40 AM UTC (Sunday).

Breakdown

The Unix timestamp 1000000000 is known as the billennium -- the moment when Unix time reached one billion seconds. It fell on a Sunday morning, September 9, 2001. In ISO 8601 format, this is 2001-09-09T01:46:40Z.
