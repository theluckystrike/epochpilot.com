What Is 10 AM CET in PST?

10 AM CET is 1 AM PST.

How to Convert

CET (Central European Time) is UTC+1 and PST (Pacific Standard Time) is UTC-8. The difference is 9 hours — PST is 9 hours behind CET.

10:00 AM CET - 9h = 1:00 AM PST

Note: During daylight saving time, Europe and the US switch on different dates, which can temporarily change the gap to 8 hours. When both regions are in summer time (CEST/PDT), the difference returns to 9 hours.
