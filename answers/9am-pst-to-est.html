What Is 9 AM PST in EST?

9 AM PST is 12 PM (noon) EST.

How to Convert

PST (Pacific Standard Time) is UTC-8 and EST (Eastern Standard Time) is UTC-5. The difference is always 3 hours — EST is 3 hours ahead of PST.

9:00 AM PST + 3h = 12:00 PM EST

During daylight saving time, both zones shift forward by one hour (PDT = UTC-7, EDT = UTC-4), so the 3-hour gap remains.
