What Is the Cron Expression for Weekdays Only?

0 9 * * 1-5

Breakdown

The 1-5 in the day-of-week field means Monday (1) through Friday (5), excluding Saturday (6) and Sunday (0).

Minute0 — at minute 0
Hour9 — at 9 AM
Day of Month* — every day
Month* — every month
Day of Week1-5 — Monday to Friday

This runs 5 times per week — once each weekday at 9:00 AM. Change the time fields to adjust the schedule.
