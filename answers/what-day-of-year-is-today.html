What Is an Ordinal Date?

The ordinal date (or day-of-year number) counts the days sequentially from January 1. It ranges from 1 to 365 in a regular year, or 1 to 366 in a leap year. It is used in ISO 8601 ordinal date format (e.g., 2026-101 for day 101 of 2026).
