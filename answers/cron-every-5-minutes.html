What Is the Cron Expression for Every 5 Minutes?

*/5 * * * *

Breakdown

The */5 in the minute field means "every 5th minute." The remaining * fields mean every hour, every day, every month, every day of the week.

Minute*/5 — every 5th minute (0, 5, 10, 15...55)
Hour* — every hour
Day of Month* — every day
Month* — every month
Day of Week* — every weekday

This runs 288 times per day (12 times per hour x 24 hours).
