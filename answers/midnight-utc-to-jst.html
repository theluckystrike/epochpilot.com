What Is Midnight UTC in JST?

Midnight UTC (12:00 AM) is 9:00 AM JST.

How to Convert

JST (Japan Standard Time) is UTC+9. To convert from UTC to JST, add 9 hours. Japan does not observe daylight saving time, so this offset is fixed year-round.

12:00 AM UTC + 9h = 9:00 AM JST
