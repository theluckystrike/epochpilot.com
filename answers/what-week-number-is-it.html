About ISO Week Numbers

ISO 8601 defines the week number system used internationally. Key rules:

  • Weeks start on Monday
  • Week 1 is the week containing the first Thursday of the year
  • A year has either 52 or 53 weeks
  • December 29-31 may belong to week 1 of the next year, and January 1-3 may belong to the last week of the previous year
