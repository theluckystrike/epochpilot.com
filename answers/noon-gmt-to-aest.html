What Is 12 PM GMT in AEST?

12 PM GMT (noon) is 10 PM AEST.

How to Convert

GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is UTC+0 and AEST (Australian Eastern Standard Time) is UTC+10. To convert from GMT to AEST, add 10 hours.

12:00 PM GMT + 10h = 10:00 PM AEST

During Australian daylight saving time (October to April), eastern Australia uses AEDT (UTC+11), which would make noon GMT equal to 11 PM AEDT.
