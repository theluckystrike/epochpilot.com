What Is 8 AM IST in UTC?

8 AM IST is 2:30 AM UTC.

How to Convert

IST (Indian Standard Time) is UTC+5:30. To convert from IST to UTC, subtract 5 hours and 30 minutes. India does not observe daylight saving time, so this offset is fixed year-round.

8:00 AM IST - 5h 30m = 2:30 AM UTC
