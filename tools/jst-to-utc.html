Frequently Asked Questions

What is the JST to UTC offset?

Japan Standard Time (JST) is UTC+9 year-round. Japan does not observe Daylight Saving Time.

How do I convert JST to EST?

JST is 14 hours ahead of EST (13 hours ahead of EDT). For example, 9 AM JST is 7 PM EST the previous day. Use this tool for exact conversions on any date.

Does Japan observe Daylight Saving Time?

No. Japan has not observed DST since 1951. JST remains at UTC+9 throughout the entire year, making timezone calculations simpler.

Is this tool free?

Yes. All EpochPilot tools are free, run in your browser, and require no signup or account.

Does this tool work offline?

Once the page is loaded, it works entirely in your browser with no internet connection needed. The initial page load requires internet.

How It Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser using vanilla JavaScript. No data is sent to any server. All conversions and calculations happen locally on your device, ensuring complete privacy.

EpochPilot uses the browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat API and the IANA timezone database provided by your operating system. This means timezone conversions are always accurate, including Daylight Saving Time transitions.
