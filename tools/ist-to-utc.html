Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IST to UTC offset?

Indian Standard Time (IST) is UTC+5:30 year-round. India does not observe Daylight Saving Time, so the offset never changes.

Why is India's timezone offset 30 minutes?

India chose UTC+5:30 as a compromise to serve the entire country with a single timezone. The half-hour offset better centers the timezone across India's geographic span from Gujarat to Arunachal Pradesh.

How do I convert IST to EST?

IST is 10.5 hours ahead of EST (or 9.5 hours ahead of EDT during summer). For example, 9 PM IST is 10:30 AM EST. Use this converter to get exact results for any date.

How It Works

This tool runs entirely in your browser using vanilla JavaScript. No data is sent to any server. All conversions and calculations happen locally on your device, ensuring complete privacy.

EpochPilot uses the browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat API and the IANA timezone database provided by your operating system. This means timezone conversions are always accurate, including Daylight Saving Time transitions.
